# Origin — Delimitation Log

**Status:** Evaluating

This file records the reasoning used to delimit the Origin construct: what counts as part of the construct, where its boundaries fall, and which candidate formulations were excluded during specification.

The current construct definition is maintained in [`origin_determinacy.md`](origin_determinacy.md).

## Delimitation 001 — Initiation Boundary

### Question

Should Origin be determined by the first human to access an account, model, interface, or chat?

### Finding

No. Chronological priority alone is insufficient because unrelated prior use can precede a later trajectory without constituting part of that trajectory.

### Resulting Boundary

A trajectory is delimited by a declared scope. Origin is determined relative to the valid historical dependency structure of that declared trajectory, not by mere prior access.

---

## Delimitation 002 — Historical Dependence

### Question

Should all prior causal or background influences count as historical dependencies of the trajectory?

### Finding

No. Unrestricted historical dependence permits indefinite regress and can make meaningful scope declaration impossible.

### Resulting Boundary

The relevant relation is **constitutive historical dependence** rather than unrestricted causal or background dependence.

A prior human contribution is constitutively historically dependent when removing that contribution from the record would cause the declared trajectory to cease being identifiable as the same trajectory, rather than merely making the trajectory different in content, quality, or direction.

Mere background influence, enabling conditions, prior access, unrelated activity, or general intellectual influence do not establish constitutive historical dependence.

---

## Delimitation 003 — Scope Relativity

### Question

Must Origin be globally unique across an account, model, interface, or complete interaction record?

### Finding

No. A single interaction record may contain multiple nested or otherwise distinct valid trajectories.

### Resulting Boundary

Origin is determinate **per valid declared trajectory**.

Multiple valid declarations may coexist. Different valid trajectories may therefore have different Origins without creating ambiguity within any one valid declaration.

## Current Delimitation

The Origin construct is defined by a valid declared trajectory, constitutive historical dependence, and attribution of the earliest human contribution on which that trajectory is constitutively historically dependent.

These sections specify the construct. They are not themselves evaluations of Claim 001's operational proposition.
